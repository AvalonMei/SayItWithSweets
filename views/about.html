My Story

Welcome to Say it with Sweets! I'm Sharon Ebersole, and this little bakery of mine, tucked away on Pleasant Valley Boulevard in Altoona, Pennsylvania, is my pride and joy. Back in 2012, I decided to turn my passion for baking into something more, and thus Say it with Sweets was born. It's been an incredible journey of over 12 years, filled with flour, sugar, and lots of love.

Here, we specialize in making your celebrations a bit sweeter with our cakes, cookies, and uniquely designed cupcake creations. While we do not have a traditional storefront (everything's made to order for pickup), each treat is crafted with the utmost care and attention to detail, just for you. My bakery is more than just a business; it's a way to connect, celebrate, and share joy through the universal language of sweets. I am thrilled to bring a piece of my heart to your table and even more excited to share our journey with you online. Thanks for stopping by, and I can't wait to create something special for you
